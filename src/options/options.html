Tab Out settings

Choose what sits at the top of your dashboard and how tabs get grouped.

Pinned sites

Always shown first on the dashboard. If a site has open tabs you get its normal card; if not, you get a click-to-open placeholder.

Homepage rules

URLs matching these rules are collected into one Homepages card, so you can clear your Gmail inbox, X feed and YouTube front page in a single click without touching the content tabs on those same sites.

Custom groups

Merge several hostnames into one card, or split one site into separate cards by path. Rules are checked top to bottom — the first match wins.

History

How many recently closed tabs to remember. The oldest entries drop off automatically once you go over this limit. Reopening a tab, or a page you already have open, is never counted as history.
